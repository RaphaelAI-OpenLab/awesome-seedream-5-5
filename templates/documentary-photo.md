An early-morning bicycle workshop. A middle-aged mechanic bends over a wheel. Cool window light meets a warm task lamp. A trace of grease on the fingers, natural apron folds and a worn workbench. ## 验收项

1. 放大检查皮肤、手指、轮辐与工具形状。
2. 检查光源方向和阴影是否一致。
3. 确认没有生成未要求的招牌或水印。
